DORA64 — quick 32/32 walkthrough
Stages are listed in route order. Visit counts cover the full collection.

0. Start — Woody
Collect: Anywhere Door on top of his head.

1. First field — 2 visits
Requires: Anywhere Door.
First: Air Cannon — chest beyond the bridge; Tanma Watch — lower recess at the path's end;
Wild Beast-Taming Pellets — recess below the floating islands. Beat Ponta: Earth piece #1.
Second: Champion Gloves — talk to the fairy before Ponta;
Mini-Dora — collect the reward for defeating him again.
Boss: shoot Ponta's belly button.

2. Pokera's valley — 2 visits
Requires: Wild Beast-Taming Pellets.
First: Safety Charm Sticker — by the poison water; Air Tubes — chest at the mouse's nest.
After collecting the tubes, return to the pond, break the barrier, free the mouse
and follow it back to its nest: Earth piece #2.
Second: Shock Wave Pistol — talk to the mouse six times, stepping away between conversations.

3. Tropical sea — 1 visit
Requires: Air Tubes.
Collect: Shrink Ray — right-hand recess before the tunnel; Gene Mic — above the tunnel exit
by the dark rock; Time Kerchief — lower channel beyond the tunnel.
Talk to the fairy by the raised shore, then its companion, to raise the water.
Collect Sea piece #1 on the newly accessible beach. Get the chests before the piece.

4. Forest cave — 1 visit
Requires: Shrink Ray.
Switches: press tree and circles below; arrows and crescent above, leaving the star alone;
press the lower circular switch again to open the other passage.
Collect: Pass Loop — beneath the waterfall; Danger Bomb — in the tree's branches.
Cross the platforms and sliding blocks, then pass the giant bird: Earth piece #3.

5. Volcano Castle — 1 visit
Requires: Pass Loop.
Defeat the guards, speak to the fairies to lower the lava, and continue.
Collect: Echo Mountain and Earth Key in the chest area. Unlock the boss door.
Boss: Minotaur — jump on his head/horns; Shizuka makes this easier.
After winning, talk to the Earth King to combine the three pieces into the Earth Stone.

6. Underwater races — 3 visits
Requires: Time Kerchief. Repairing the vehicle registers Underwater Buggy.
First victory: Deep Sea Cream.
Second victory: Sea piece #2. Pick up Upgrade Spray on the course for its entry.
Third victory: What-If Phone Booth.

7. Ghost ship — 1 visit
Requires: Deep Sea Cream.
Collect: Jack Bean — inside the ship beyond the crabs; activate the walkway switch.
Picture Book Entry Shoes — on the ledge reached using that walkway.
Knock down three stalactites to raise the water. Activate the switch behind the waterfall.
Adapting Ray — lower hull passage after raising the ship.
Boss: skeleton captain — shoot when he materializes. Reward: Sea piece #3.

8. Undersea Castle — 1 visit
Requires: Adapting Ray.
Get the Sea Key behind the library shelves; cross the moving platforms to the boss door.
Boss: Big Santa — dodge the snow attacks and shoot. Reward: Mobile Fishing Pond.
Talk to the Sea King to restore the Sea Stone from its three pieces.

9. Sky plains — 1 visit
Requires: Jack Bean — grow the beanstalk near Pokera and climb to the Sky hub.
Collect: Translation Gummy — recess below the first sinking bridge section;
Anything Controller — lower island by the last section;
Sonic Solidifier — at the tree, complete the fairy's request by defeating the owls.
Boss: harpy — attack as she approaches. Reward: Sky piece #1.

10. Roc flight — 2 visits
Requires: Anything Controller.
First ascent to the nest: Sky piece #2.
Second ascent: Hopter.

11. Stormy sky — 2 visits
Requires: Hopter. Rest on islands to recharge.
First: Extra Hold Cloud Spray — from the fairy; defeat both owls on the moon and return
to the nearby island for Cordless Can Phone. Collect Sky piece #3 on the moon.
Second: Camping Capsule — on the moon after its piece has been collected.
If you missed Cordless Can Phone on the first visit, collect it on the return.

12. Sky Castle — 1 visit
Requires: Extra Hold Cloud Spray.
Collect: Instant Wardrobe Cam — second chest from the left in the upper row of five;
Sky Key — outside moving platforms; Time TV — fairy on the cloud below the outer wall
near the locked door. Collect everything, then use the key to open the door.
Boss: Centaur — dodge his charges to make him hit the wall; attack when he turns red.
Talk to the Sky King to restore the Sky Stone from its three pieces.

13. Finale
With all three stones restored, enter the light portal near Woody.
Demon King: dodge his attacks and shoot the pendant on his chest.
Watch the ending until the title sequence returns to unlock Corona.

Total: 32 gadgets, 9 pieces, 3 restored Spirit Stones and 3 castle keys.
Tanma Watch, Safety Charm Sticker, Upgrade Spray and Mini-Dora count toward the 32.
